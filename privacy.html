Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Pitoka is a children’s learning app provided by Bzhar Kaka. This policy explains how information is handled in the app and on its support website. Parents and guardians can contact pitoka.support@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Learning and settings on your device

Pitoka stores learning progress and language preferences on your device. Tracing gestures are processed in the app; we do not upload drawings, tracing paths, or learning progress to our own server. Pitoka does not require an account, and it does not ask children for their name, email address, birthday, photos, contacts, or location.

Purchases and RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to verify purchases, unlock paid content, and restore access. When billing is initialized, RevenueCat assigns a random app user identifier and processes purchase and entitlement information, such as product identifiers, transaction records, and purchase status. It also processes technical information needed to operate billing, such as app and operating-system versions, device type, locale, and service connection information. Billing connections can occur when the app opens, even before a purchase.

Apple or Google handles payment through the relevant store. Pitoka does not receive your full payment-card details. RevenueCat and the store process information under their respective policies. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy, Apple’s Privacy Policy, and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Children, advertising, and speech

The app has no ads, advertising tracking, chat, or social profiles. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Purchases are intended for adults and are placed behind a parent arithmetic gate. We use purchase-related information to operate and support paid access.

Spoken learning words use the device’s text-to-speech service. Pitoka does not record the microphone or a child’s voice. The selected speech engine may process the app’s learning text according to the device provider’s settings and privacy policy.

Support messages and website visits

If you email us, we receive your address and the information you send, and use it to answer your request. Please do not send a child’s personal information unless needed to resolve the issue. Support email is handled through Gmail.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the website. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local learning data remains until removed from the device; operating-system backups may retain a copy under your backup settings. Removing local app data does not delete purchase records held by the store or RevenueCat.

We retain support correspondence as needed to address requests and resolve follow-up issues. Purchase providers retain records under their policies and applicable obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information we control, including information you believe a child provided. We may need information to locate a record and verify the request. Some transaction records may need to be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location.

Service providers and security

Information may be processed in countries other than your own by the providers described above. We limit our use to operating and supporting Pitoka, and may disclose information when required by law. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. For support or privacy requests, email pitoka.support@gmail.com.
